## Full Description
```text
Sakreen Shot is a fast, private, 100% offline screenshot organizer designed for Pakistani retail, wholesale, supplier, payment, customer support, and personal workflows.

Tired of digging through thousands of unorganized screenshots to find an old JazzCash receipt, EasyPaisa confirmation, customer chat, CNIC copy, or delivery order? Sakreen Shot automatically organizes your screenshot library locally on your phone and makes every text inside your screenshots searchable instantly.

100% LOCAL PRIVACY GUARANTEE
• No Internet Required: Sakreen Shot operates entirely offline and does not request internet permissions.
• Zero Cloud Processing: All OCR text recognition and auto-categorization happen locally on your device.
• No Tracking & No Ads: Zero analytics, zero ad SDKs, and zero background data collection.
• Android Auto-Backup Disabled: Your sensitive screenshot text (CNICs, receipts, chats) is never synced to Google Drive.

KEY FEATURES

1. SMART LOCAL CATEGORIZATION
Automatically detects and sorts screenshots into functional categories:
- Payments (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Receipts)
- Chats & Support (WhatsApp, Customer Inquiries)
- ID & Official Documents (CNIC copies, Forms, Tax Documents)
- Delivery & Orders (Logistics, Tracking, Invoices)
- Work & Notes (Task lists, Product Codes)
- Unsorted (Everything else requiring quick review)

2. INSTANT ON-DEVICE OCR & FULL-TEXT SEARCH
Find any screenshot in milliseconds. Type a customer name, transaction reference ID, mobile number, bank title, or product code, and Sakreen Shot instantly finds the matching screenshot using on-device text recognition.

3. USER-CONTROLLED CLEANUP & DELETION
Free up valuable phone storage without risk. Select disposable screenshots and delete them cleanly. Deletion uses official Android system confirmation dialogs (Scoped Storage), ensuring you are always in total control.

4. PINNING & RE-CLASSIFICATION
Pin critical receipts or customer confirmations for instant access. Easily change categories with a single tap.

BUILT FOR LOCAL BUSINESS & DAILY CONVENIENCE
Whether you manage a retail shop, handle wholesale billing, process daily mobile payments, or just want a clean gallery, Sakreen Shot brings calm organization and total privacy to your digital record-keeping.

Note: Sakreen Shot reconciles newly captured screenshots locally upon app open or via local background background sync. System battery-saver settings may vary background sync timing across Android manufacturers.
```
