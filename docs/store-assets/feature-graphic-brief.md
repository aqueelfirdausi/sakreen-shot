# Sakreen Shot — Play Store Feature Graphic Brief

This design brief specifies exact requirements for generating or producing the official Google Play Store Feature Graphic.

---

## Technical Specifications
- **Dimensions**: 1024 × 500 pixels
- **Aspect Ratio**: ~2:1
- **File Format**: PNG or high-quality JPEG (no transparency)
- **Max File Size**: 15 MB

---

## Visual Direction & Brand Guidelines

### Color Palette
- **Primary Background**: Warm parchment / luxury ivory (`#F6F2EB` to `#FAF7F2`)
- **Primary Typography & Linework**: Deep espresso (`#2A2421`)
- **Secondary Text & Sub-elements**: Muted espresso (`#5C524A`)
- **Accent Details**: Restrained bronze (`#8C6D46` / `#9E7B4F`)
- **Card Surfaces**: Soft warm ivory (`#FAF7F2`) with subtle 1px espresso/bronze borders

### Graphical Elements
- **Central Symbol**: A refined stacked archive card motif with subtle crop-corner brackets (matching the launcher icon symbol).
- **Subtle Texture**: Ultra-fine paper grain finish across the background (no dirty paper, no stains, no torn edges, no rustic vintage distress).
- **Typography**: Clean, elegant serif or editorial sans-serif (e.g. Outfit, Playfair Display, or Inter) for the headline.

### Content & Wording
- **Title**: `Sakreen Shot`
- **Subtitle**: `Private Offline Screenshot Organizer`
- **Zero Fake Screenshots**: Do NOT place fake phone mockups with messy interface content inside the feature graphic.
- **Zero Unsupported Claims**: Do NOT use buzzwords like "AI-Powered", "Cloud Intelligence", "Military Grade Encryption", or "Instant Bank Sync".

---

## Layout Composition
1. **Left / Center**: Large, clean `Sakreen Shot` brand title in deep espresso with subtitle in muted espresso/bronze.
2. **Right**: Stacked parchment record cards symbol with restrained bronze corner motifs and a subtle search glass icon outline.
3. **Margin Safe Zone**: Keep text and key visual marks within the central 800 × 400 area to prevent crop clipping across different Play Store display viewports.
